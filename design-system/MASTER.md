# Design System Master for Iskin Clinic

Based on **UI-UX Pro Max** intelligence.

## 1. Product Context
- **Product Type**: Healthcare & Medical Service (Cosmetic Clinic)
- **Target Audience**: C-end users (Beauty & Wellness seekers)
- **Tone**: Trustworthy, Premium, Clean, Professional

## 2. Style Recommendations
- **Primary Style**: **Clean Minimalism**
- **Secondary Accents**: **Glassmorphism** (for interactive cards and overlays)
- **Pattern**: **Grid Layout** (structured and organized)

## 3. Color Palette
- **Primary**: `#005043` (Deep Evergreen - Trust & Sophistication)
- **Secondary**: `#D4AF37` (Gold - Premium Accent)
- **Background**: `#F9FAFB` (Soft Gray/White - Medical Cleanliness)
- **Text**: `#111827` (Deep Slate - High Contrast/Readability)

## 4. Typography Pairing
- **Headings**: **Outfit** (Modern, Geometric, Professional)
- **Body**: **Inter** (Excellent legibility for medical info)

## 5. Interaction Patterns
- **Buttons**: Semi-rounded (8px) with subtle elevation.
- **Feedback**: Immediate 150ms opacity transition on hover/tap.
- **Safe Areas**: Strict adherence to notch and bottom indicators for mobile patients.

## 6. UX Guidelines
- **Loading**: Use shimmering skeletons for medical record states.
- **Trust Indicators**: Clear display of certifications and "Before/After" results with high-quality imagery.
